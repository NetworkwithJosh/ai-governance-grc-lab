# AI Risk Register — Practice Examples

The lab used the structure **Threat → Vulnerability → Impact → Asset**.

## Risk 1 — Recruiting Hallucination / Overreliance
AI hallucination combined with overreliance on automated scoring may cause qualified candidates to be incorrectly rejected during the recruiting and hiring process.

## Risk 2 — Chatbot Availability Failure
A broken chatbot integration combined with the lack of a failover system may cause support tickets to become backlogged during the IT troubleshooting process.

## Risk 3 — SIEM Misclassification
An AI-enabled SIEM model may incorrectly classify activity when endpoint events are missing, leading to incorrect remediation or missed security events.

## Risk 4 — IDS/IPS False Positives
An AI/ML-enabled IDS/IPS may misclassify legitimate traffic as malicious because of poor model tuning, blocking valid traffic and making protected applications or services unavailable.

## Risk 5 — Fraud Model False Positives
An AI fraud-detection model may produce inaccurate anomaly scores when transaction data is incomplete, outdated, or biased, causing legitimate activity to be flagged as fraudulent.

## Risk 6 — Generative AI Sensitive Data Exposure
A generative AI chatbot may expose confidential internal or customer information if access controls, data permissions, or knowledge-source restrictions are weak.
