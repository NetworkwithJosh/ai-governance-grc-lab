# AI Controls

1. Require human review of AI-generated candidate recommendations before hiring decisions.
2. Require quarterly validation of AI recruiting models for bias, accuracy, and adverse decision patterns.
3. Restrict AI systems to approved data sources using role-based access controls.
4. Require sensitive data filtering before information is submitted to generative AI systems.
5. Require AI chatbot responses to reference approved knowledge sources for high-impact support guidance.
6. Maintain a human support failover process for AI chatbot outages.
7. Monitor AI-enabled SIEM and IDS/IPS detection models for false positives, false negatives, missing telemetry, and degradation.
8. Require documented testing and approval before production AI model, prompt, configuration, or integration changes.
9. Maintain centralized logging of AI activity, administrative changes, authentication events, and security events.
10. Perform periodic AI incident-response exercises covering data leakage, hallucinations, model failure, prompt injection, unauthorized access, and compromised integrations.

## Control Design Principles

Each control should be:

- Specific
- Measurable
- Testable
- Assigned to an accountable owner
- Performed at a defined frequency
- Supported by evidence
