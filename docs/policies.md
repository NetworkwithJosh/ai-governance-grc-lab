# AI Governance Policies

## 1. AI Governance and Human Oversight Policy
Establishes human-review, accountability, model-validation, and high-impact decision requirements.

**Owner:** AI Governance Lead

## 2. AI Data Protection and Access Control Policy
Establishes least privilege, approved data sources, sensitive-data filtering, and AI data-access requirements.

**Owner:** Information Security Manager

## 3. AI Security Monitoring, Logging, and Change Management Policy
Establishes logging, monitoring, model/configuration change controls, and security-detection assurance requirements.

**Owner:** Security Operations Manager

## 4. AI Resilience and Incident Response Policy
Establishes failover, business continuity, AI incident response, and periodic testing requirements.

**Owner:** Information Security Manager / IT Operations Manager
